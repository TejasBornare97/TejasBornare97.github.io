How I build

AI you can check

Q: "What is the escape of water excess under the Harbourline policy?"

Documents become labelled chunks

PDFs are parsed with headers and footers removed, table rows rebuilt so a figure stays next to its label, and split only within sections. Every chunk is stamped with its insurer, product and section, so two lookalike tables can't be confused.

From: Policy RAG Assistant

Chunks

Harbourline | Home | Part 5Escape of water ........ £400
Northgate | Home | Section 7Escape of water ........ £500
Harbourline | Home | Part 5Every claim unless shown below ........ £350

Meaning and keywords, fused

Vector search finds passages about the topic. BM25 rewards exact words like "excess". Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges both lists, and filters keep the search to the insurer the question names.

From: Policy RAG Assistant

Fused ranking

Harbourline Part 5: excess table#1
Harbourline Part 2: escape of water cover#2
Harbourline definitions#3
Northgate Section 7 (filtered)out

Answers only from numbered sources

The model gets labelled, numbered passages and strict rules: cite every fact, quote figures exactly, never mix insurers. Invented source numbers are dropped before anything reaches the user.

From: Policy RAG Assistant

Answer

The escape of water excess under the Harbourline household policy is £400 1

1 Harbourline Insurance, Home Policy Wording, Part 5

Trust, but check in code

Prompts aren't enough. In testing, the model once invented a £5,000,000 limit. Now every £ amount and percentage in an answer must appear in its cited text, or the answer is flagged.

From: Policy RAG Assistant

Checks

✓Citation [1] maps to a retrieved passage
✓£400 found in cited text
✓Only one insurer referenced
✕Earlier test: £5,000,000 not in any source, flagged red

Grey areas go to a person

When the documents don't settle a question, the system says so, cites the closest clauses and recommends referral, the way a claims handler would. The same idea runs through my client work: AI drafts, people decide.

From: Policy RAG & client work
